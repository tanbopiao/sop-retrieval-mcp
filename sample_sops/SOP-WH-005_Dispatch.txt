SOP-WH-005: DISPATCH AND LOADING PROCEDURE
Version: 2.2
Effective Date: 2026-09-20

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines the checks and actions required before goods leave the warehouse on outbound vehicles.

2. BEFORE LOADING
2.1 Verify the vehicle temperature is at range before loading chilled or frozen orders. Chilled: 2 to 8 degrees Celsius. Frozen: minus 18 degrees Celsius or below.
2.2 Confirm the route manifest matches the vehicle number. Loading against the wrong manifest is a critical error and must be reported the same day.

3. LOADING SEQUENCE
3.1 Load in reverse drop order: the last delivery is loaded first.
3.2 Heavy cartons on the floor, light cartons on top. Do not stack above the load line marked inside the vehicle.
3.3 Seal the vehicle after loading and record the seal number on the manifest.

4. PRE-DEPARTURE CHECKS
4.1 The dispatcher must confirm all cartons on the manifest are loaded. Any missing carton blocks departure until located.
4.2 Hand the driver the signed manifest and the temperature log printout for chilled loads.

5. WHAT MUST BE CHECKED BEFORE DISPATCHING GOODS - SUMMARY
Before dispatching goods, the operator must verify: vehicle temperature at range, route manifest matches the vehicle, all cartons loaded, seal applied and recorded, and driver documentation handed over. Dispatch is not permitted with any open food-safety hold or quality hold flag on the order.
