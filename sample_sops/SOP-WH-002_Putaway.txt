SOP-WH-002: PUTAWAY PROCEDURE
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: 2026-08-10

1. PURPOSE
This procedure covers movement of received goods from the receiving staging lane to their storage locations.

2. TASK ASSIGNMENT
2.1 Putaway tasks are assigned by the system in the putaway queue. Operators must work tasks in the sequence displayed.
2.2 Do not put away goods that show a red DAMAGE tag. Route them to the damage review area Q3 instead.

3. LOCATION RULES
3.1 Chilled goods must be placed in cold rooms CR1 or CR2 only. Never stage chilled goods at ambient locations, even temporarily.
3.2 Heavy items above 15 kg per carton must go to ground-level locations (aisles A and B, levels 1-2).
3.3 Fast-moving SKUs flagged with a yellow location label must be placed in pick faces facing aisle C.

4. CONFIRMATION
4.1 Scan the pallet label and the location label to confirm putaway in the system.
4.2 If the system proposes a location that is full, request an alternative from the shift supervisor. Do not place goods in an unassigned location.

5. EXCEPTIONS
5.1 Mixed pallets discovered during putaway must be split at the rework bench R2 before storage.
