SOP-WH-004: PACKING AND LABELLING PROCEDURE
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2026-07-15

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines packing standards and label verification before cartons leave the packing area.

2. CARTON SELECTION
2.1 Use the carton size proposed by the system. If the proposed size is unavailable, use the next larger size with void fill; never force items into a smaller carton.
2.2 Chilled orders must use insulated cartons with two gel packs. Frozen orders use insulated cartons with dry ice, handled only by trained staff.

3. LABEL VERIFICATION
3.1 Scan every carton label before sealing. The system must confirm the carton contents match the order.
3.2 Export orders require the shipping mark label in addition to the standard carton label. Missing shipping marks must be reported to the export desk before sealing.

4. SEALING AND STAGING
4.1 Apply tape in an H pattern for cartons above 5 kg.
4.2 Stage sealed cartons on the outbound lane matching the delivery route. Cartons staged on the wrong lane must be moved within 15 minutes.

5. WEIGHT CHECK
5.1 Cartons are weighed automatically at the lane exit. A variance above 5 percent from the expected weight blocks the carton and requires repacking.
